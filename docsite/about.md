# Citing and acknowledgements

## How to cite MRRpy

MRRpy took a long time to get right and is given away free and open source. If
you use it in your work, please cite it:

> Bhattarai, S., Pradhan, N. R., and Talchabhadel, R. (2026). *MRRpy: A modular
> Python model for distributed rainfall–runoff, flood routing, and inundation
> mapping* (version 1.0.0) [Computer software].
> <https://github.com/SauravBhattarai19/MRRpy>

```bibtex
@software{bhattarai2026mrrpy,
  author  = {Bhattarai, Saurav and Pradhan, Nawa Raj and Talchabhadel, Rocky},
  title   = {{MRRpy}: A modular {Python} model for distributed rainfall--runoff,
             flood routing, and inundation mapping},
  year    = {2026},
  version = {1.0.0},
  url     = {https://github.com/SauravBhattarai19/MRRpy}
}
```

The *Cite this repository* button on the
[GitHub page](https://github.com/SauravBhattarai19/MRRpy) gives the same
reference in other formats. A paper describing MRRpy has been written for the
*Journal of Open Source Software*; its reference will be added here once it is
published.

MRRpy was developed as part of Saurav Bhattarai's PhD research in the Department
of Civil and Environmental Engineering at Jackson State University (JSU), and it
is one chapter of that dissertation. It grew out of a research collaboration
between the U.S. Army Engineer Research and Development Center, Coastal and
Hydraulics Laboratory (ERDC–CHL), and the Hydrology Laboratory at JSU.

This research was supported in part by an appointment to the Department of
Defense (DOD) Research Participation Program administered by the Oak Ridge
Institute for Science and Education (ORISE) through an interagency agreement
between the U.S. Department of Energy (DOE) and the DOD. ORISE is managed by
ORAU under DOE contract number DE-SC0014664. It was also supported by ERDC–CHL
through its Earth System Hydrology research initiative, with technical support
provided under the ERDC–CHL professional services contract with Taylor–Woolpert,
LLC. All opinions expressed here are the authors' and do not necessarily reflect
the policies and views of DOD, DOE, ERDC, or ORAU/ORISE.

MRRpy is built on the open-source scientific-Python and geospatial ecosystem
(NumPy, SciPy, pandas, Matplotlib, rasterio, GeoPandas, pysheds, pyflwdir) and
on the Google Earth Engine and Open-Meteo data services. We thank their
developers and maintainers.
